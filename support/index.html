Support

Get help or report a problem

Load Pay is built by one person, so email goes to the person who can fix it.

From inside the app

Open Pay setup and tap Send feedback. It opens an email with the app version, platform and driver type already filled in at the bottom, which is the first thing needed to reproduce a bug.

By email

Write to huntercbarnett@gmail.com. Screenshots help, and for pay questions the load's numbers and your Pay setup choices help most.

Before you email about a pay figure

Check the load's pay breakdown, which shows every line and the rule in effect, and the FAQ on how extras are handled. Most surprises are the accessorial setting.

Lost data

If you made a backup from Pay setup, restore it from the same screen on the new device. If you did not, data cannot be recovered from us, because we never had it.

Load Pay is built by one person, Hunter Barnett, for drivers who want to know what a week actually paid. Nothing you log leaves your phone. Suggestions and bug reports are welcome at huntercbarnett@gmail.com.
