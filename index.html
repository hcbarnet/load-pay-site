Load Pay is a load and pay tracker for truck drivers. Log each load, see completed and projected gross against your weekly goal, check every settlement against what you were owed, and, if you run your own truck, see net after fuel and fixed costs. No account, no subscription, nothing leaves your phone.

Why this exists

Pay apps for drivers tend to be spreadsheets with a logo.

A driver on percentage pay needs three numbers a week: what the loads should pay, what the settlement actually paid, and the gap. Owner-operators need a fourth: what was left after the truck. Load Pay is built around those numbers and not much else, so logging a load takes under a minute and the dashboard answers the question before you ask it.
