Privacy Policy for Settle Sheet

Last updated: September 18, 2026

The short version: Settle Sheet does not collect, transmit or sell your information. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, and no analytics. Everything you log stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

Who we are

Settle Sheet is developed by Hunter Barnett, an individual developer. Contact: huntercbarnett@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own. We do not receive your loads, pay figures, expenses, photos, settings or anything else you enter.

Where your information lives

Loads, settlements, expenses, templates and settings are stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. Photos you attach are copied into the app's documents folder on the device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Standard device backups made by iOS or Android may include it under your own Apple or Google account, governed by their policies.

City suggestions

When you type a city, suggestions come from a list bundled inside the app and from cities you have used before. Only if that list has no match does the app send the text you typed to Photon, an OpenStreetMap-based geocoding service run by komoot, to look for smaller places. That request contains the typed text and no other information about you. You can avoid it entirely by typing the full "City, ST" yourself.

Distance estimates

Estimated miles are calculated on the device from bundled city coordinates. No location services are used and the app never asks for your location.

Camera and photo access

If you attach a photo to a load, the app asks for camera or photo library access at that moment. Photos are stored only on the device and are never uploaded by the app.

Backup, export and sharing

Backups, CSV files, PDF statements and feedback emails are created on request and handed to the system share sheet or your mail app. Where they go from there is your choice, and that destination's policies apply.

Children's privacy

Settle Sheet is intended for working drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever adds a feature that changes any of the above, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: huntercbarnett@gmail.com.

Settle Sheet is built by one person, Hunter Barnett, for drivers who want to know what a week actually paid. Nothing you log leaves your phone. Suggestions and bug reports are welcome at huntercbarnett@gmail.com.
