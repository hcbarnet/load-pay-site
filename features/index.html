Features

Everything in the app, in the order you meet it.

Built for drivers on percentage, per-mile or flat pay, with an owner-operator mode that adds costs and net.

Dashboard

Completed gross for the pay week, projected total including loads in progress, a pace bar against your weekly goal, miles, average $/mi, and the week's loads. Hold a load to run it again.

Add a load

Form or three-step wizard. Cities suggest instantly as you type, routes can carry stops, and accessorials are chips: detention, stop-offs, tarp, layover, lumper, plus any custom extra. Pay updates as you type.

Saved loads

Save a lane you run often, with its usual miles, linehaul and extras, and fill the whole form in one tap next time.

Settlement reconciliation

For any pay week, enter what each load actually paid and see the difference against what it should have. Mark the week settled with the statement total and date. If the statement matches the other accessorial rule to the cent, the app says so and offers a one-tap fix.

History

Weeks, months, quarters and years with goal progress and settlement status, plus search by load ID or city.

Insights

Weekly trend against goal over 8 weeks to a year, year to date and year over year, deadhead ratio, accessorial share, top lanes by $/mi, and settlement health.

Owner-operator mode

Fuel with gallons and odometer for price per gallon and MPG, tolls, maintenance and other expenses, optionally tied to a load. Fixed weekly costs apply to every week. Net shows on the dashboard, in history, on each load, and in Insights with cost per mile and break-even.

Export

CSV for a week, for everything, or for expenses. A settlement-style PDF for the week or the year, shared straight from the app.

Backup and restore

One zip with all your data and photos, saved wherever you like through the share sheet, restored on a new phone with the file picker.

Light and dark

Follows the system or set it yourself. Numbers stay tabular so columns line up.

Settle Sheet is built by one person, Hunter Barnett, for drivers who want to know what a week actually paid. Nothing you log leaves your phone. Suggestions and bug reports are welcome at huntercbarnett@gmail.com.
