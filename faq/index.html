FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is percentage pay calculated?

Your share is applied to the linehaul on each load. Extras such as detention are paid in full by default, because that is how most carriers pay them. If your carrier takes its percentage from extras too, turn that on in Pay setup and every load recalculates. Lumper is always reimbursed in full.

How do I know which extras rule my carrier uses?

Reconcile one week. Enter the statement total on the week's Settlement screen. If it matches the other rule to the cent, the app tells you and offers to switch.

Where do the miles come from?

From you, ideally from the rate confirmation, since those are the miles pay is based on. For a lane you have never run, the app offers an estimate from city coordinates as a starting point. It is labelled as an estimate and only appears when the field is empty.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. The only optional network call is a lookup for small towns the bundled city list does not know.

Is there an account or a subscription?

Neither. Your data lives in a database on your phone. Make a backup from Pay setup to keep a copy or move to a new device.

Does it sync between my phone and tablet?

Not yet. Backup and restore moves everything between devices; live sync may come later.

I am a company driver. Do I have to see expenses?

No. Expenses, fixed costs and net only appear when Driver type is set to Owner-operator in Pay setup.

What is the pay week?

Whatever day your carrier's pay period starts. Set it in Pay setup and every week, total and settlement follows it.
